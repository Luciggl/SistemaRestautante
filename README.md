Web System for Restaurant Management

The web-based restaurant system aims to facilitate the management of the establishment and improve the customer experience. The platform will allow customers to browse the menu, view available products, place orders, and track the status of their requests in a simple and efficient way.

The system will feature a digital menu, organized into categories such as appetizers, main courses, beverages, and desserts. Each product may include a name, description, price, image, and availability status.

Customers will be able to create an account or place orders as guests, add products to their shopping cart, select their preferences, and complete their orders. During checkout, they will be able to provide information such as their name, phone number, and delivery address, or choose the option to pick up their order at the restaurant.

For employees, the system will provide an administrative dashboard where they can add, edit, and remove products, change prices, and manage item availability. Employees will also be able to view submitted orders and update their statuses, such as Received, Preparing, Ready, and Completed.

The administrator will have access to user, product, category, and order management, as well as sales information. The system may also generate reports to assist in monitoring the restaurant's performance.

The application will be developed using web technologies, with an architecture that allows communication between the frontend and backend through an API. This will allow the system to be integrated with other services in the future, such as mobile applications, payment systems, and delivery platforms.

Main Features
Customer registration and login;
Digital menu;
Product search and filtering;
Shopping cart;
Order placement;
Delivery or pickup options;
Order status tracking;
Product registration and management;
Category management;
Product availability control;
Order management;
Administrative dashboard;
User management;
Sales reports;
Payment method integration.

The main objective of the system is to centralize and automate the restaurant's processes, reducing manual work, organizing orders, and providing customers with a faster and more convenient experience.
